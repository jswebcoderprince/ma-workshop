# MA Workshop: owner's guide

How to use the workshop planner day to day. The app is in Lithuanian, so button names are given in Lithuanian
first, with the English name in brackets.

Address: **https://workshop.maenginelab.lt** (works on a computer, tablet or phone)

---

## 1. Sign in and install on your phone

- Open the address and sign in with your e-mail and password. You can change the password in
  **Nustatymai → Paskyra** (Settings → Account).
- **Phone (iPhone):** open the address in Safari → Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Phone (Android):** open it in Chrome → menu ⋮ → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The app then opens full screen with the MA icon, like any other app.

## 2. How time works: quarter days

Every working day is split into **quarters of 2.5 hours**. Jobs are booked in quarters.

| Day | Quarters |
|---|---|
| Monday – Friday | 08:00, 10:30, 13:00, 15:30 (4 quarters) |
| Saturday | 09:00, 11:30 (2 quarters) |
| Sunday | closed |

A job's length is shown as days: ¼ d = one quarter, ½ d = half a day, 1 d = a full weekday, 2 d = two days.
You can change the opening hours in **Nustatymai → Darbo laikas** (Settings → Working hours).

**The most important rule:** the app never moves a booking by itself. If two jobs end up at the same time, it
only shows a red warning. You decide what to move.

## 3. The screen

- **Menu on the left:** **Apžvalga** (Overview), **Kalendorius** (Calendar), **Darbai** (Jobs), **Klientai**
  (Customers), **Mechanikai** (Mechanics), **Sąskaitos** (Invoices), **Nustatymai** (Settings).
  Hover over a menu item to see a small menu with its parts, for example the calendar views or the job lists.
  Hover over **Mechanikai** to see who works today and how full their day is.
- **Search** at the top (or press Ctrl K): type a customer's name, phone number or number plate.
- **Bell** at the top: customers you still need to call (see section 7).
- On a phone the menu is at the bottom of the screen.

**Apžvalga** (Overview) is the start page: jobs today, overlaps, customers to call, each mechanic's day, and a
**Reikia dėmesio** (Needs attention) list with everything that needs a decision.

## 4. Book a job

There are three ways:

1. **Naujas darbas** (New job) button, or press **N** on the calendar.
2. **Click an empty spot** on the calendar: the form opens with that mechanic, day and time already filled in.
3. **Rasti laiką** (Find a slot): choose the vehicle type and how long the job takes. The app shows the earliest
   free time for each mechanic who works on that type. Click **Rezervuoti** (Book).

In the form:

- **Klientas** (Customer): start typing a name, phone or plate to pick an existing customer, or click
  **Naujas klientas** (New customer) and enter the name, phone and vehicle.
- **Atliekami darbai** (Work to be done): what needs doing. This is printed on the job card.
- **Kada ir kas** (When and who): length, mechanic, date and start time. The box below shows the earliest free
  time; click **Naudoti** (Use) to take it. A red message means the time overlaps another job or the mechanic is
  off. You can still book it.

## 5. The calendar

- **Diena / Savaitė / Mėnuo** (Day / Week / Month) at the top left; arrows and **Šiandien** (Today) to move
  through time; the mechanic filter on the right.
- One row per mechanic. Bar colours:

| Bar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| White | Scheduled |
| Light blue | In progress |
| Amber stripes | Waiting for parts (does not block the mechanic's time) |
| Grey | Completed |
| **Red** | Overlaps another job, or the mechanic is off during part of it |
| Grey stripes | Day off, holiday or workshop closed |

- Hover over a bar to see the details. **Click a bar** to open the job panel on the right.

## 6. Change a job

### Move it

- **Drag the bar** to another day, time or mechanic (on a tablet: press and hold, then drag). A dashed outline shows
  where it will land; it turns red if it would overlap. Let go to move it; press **Esc** to cancel.
- Or open the job and press **Perkelti** (Move), then pick the date, time and mechanic.
- After a move, a message appears at the bottom with **Atšaukti** (Undo) to put the job back.
- Moving a job to **another date** marks the customer as "to call" (section 7). Changing only the mechanic does not.

### Make it longer or shorter

In the job panel, use **−¼, +¼, +½, +1d, +2d**. Other jobs are **not** moved; if the longer job now overlaps
something, it turns red and you decide.

### Status

In the job panel: **Suplanuota** (Scheduled), **Vykdoma** (In progress), **Laukiama detalių** (Waiting for parts),
**Atlikta** (Completed).

When a job in progress finishes early, press **Atlikta dabar (likęs laikas atlaisvinamas)** (Completed now): the
job is cut at the current quarter, and the rest of the time becomes free.

### Could start earlier

When time frees up, the app suggests jobs that could start earlier. You see them in the job panel and in
**Reikia dėmesio** (Needs attention) as **Galima pradėti anksčiau** (Could start earlier), with a
**Perkelti į …** (Move to …) button. Nothing moves until you press it.

## 7. Customers to call

When a booking moves to another date, the customer needs to know:

- **Perkelta vėliau** (Moved later): tell the customer the new date.
- **Perkelta anksčiau** (Moved earlier): offer the earlier date.

You find them under the bell, in **Reikia dėmesio** (Needs attention) and in **Darbai → Reikia paskambinti**
(Jobs → Customers to call). Press **Skambinti** (Call) to call from your phone, then **Pažymėti: informuotas**
(Mark informed).

## 8. Job card and invoice

### Job card (for the mechanic)

Open the job and press the printer icon, or **Darbo kortelė** (Job card) on the job page. It prints on A4 with the
customer, vehicle, work to be done, dates, and empty lines for parts, hours and signatures. No prices.

### Invoice

1. Open the job page: in the job panel press **Detalės ir sąskaita** (Parts & invoice).
2. Under **Darbai ir detalės** (Work and parts), add one line per item: choose **Darbas** (Labour) or
   **Detalė** (Part), write the description, quantity and price without VAT, press **Pridėti** (Add).
   Labour uses the hourly rate from Settings.
3. Press **Išrašyti sąskaitą MAE-…** (Issue invoice). The invoice gets the next number and the lines are locked.
4. **Spausdinti / PDF** (Print or PDF): print it, or choose "Save as PDF" in the print window to e-mail it.

An issued invoice cannot be changed. If something is wrong, press **Anuliuoti sąskaitą** (Void invoice). Its
number stays used (so the numbering has no gaps); fix the lines and issue a new invoice with the next number.
All invoices are listed under **Sąskaitos** (Invoices).

## 9. Customers

**Klientai** (Customers): all customers with their phone, vehicles, jobs and next visit. Search by name, phone or
plate. Customers are added automatically when you book a job; you can also use **Pridėti klientą** (Add customer).
Open a customer to edit details, add vehicles (**Pridėti transporto priemonę**), or add company details for invoices.

## 10. Mechanics, time off, closed days

On the **Mechanikai** (Mechanics) page:

- **Pridėti mechaniką** (Add mechanic): name, colour, what they work on (car, motorcycle, watercraft) and their
  **Darbo dienos** (Working days). A mechanic with jobs cannot be deleted; set them **Neaktyvus** (Inactive) instead.
- **Pridėti neatvykimą** (Add time off): **Atostogos** (Holiday), **Laisvadienis** (Day off) or
  **Nedarbingumas** (Sick leave), with dates. If jobs already fall in that time, the app tells you how many; they
  turn red on the calendar and you move them.
- **Dirbtuvės nedirba** (Workshop closed): days when nobody works, such as public holidays. Lithuanian public holidays
  are already added.

## 11. Settings

| Page | What to set |
|---|---|
| **Bendra** (General) | Language (Lietuvių / English), accent colour, light or dark look on this device |
| **Įmonė** (Company) | Company name, company code, VAT code, address, bank account, phone, e-mail. Printed on invoices and job cards |
| **Sąskaitos** (Invoices) | VAT payer yes/no, VAT rate, series (MAE), next number, payment term, hourly rate |
| **Darbo laikas** (Working hours) | Opening time and number of quarters for each weekday |
| **Paskyra** (Account) | Your name, e-mail and password |

## 12. On the phone

- The calendar shows a list per day instead of the grid.
- Tap a job to open it. Use **Perkelti** (Move) to move it (dragging is for computers and tablets).
- **Skambinti** (Call) calls the customer directly.
