Motion

Short and quiet (120–400 ms), only transform and opacity. Off when the system asks for less motion.

  • Sidebarrows slide in once; the active pill glides; side menus spring out row by row
  • Pagesblocks rise in one after another; card grids and table rows stagger; KPI numbers count up
  • Calendarbars fade in per range; drag and drop with a gliding outline; the bar glides to its new place
  • Feedbackload bars fill from the left; counters pop; the bell rings once; toasts spring up with Undo
